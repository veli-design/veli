# Popover

<DocsTab id="overview">

A popover shows extra help or a few controls next to the button that opened it, without leaving the page. Use it for short, optional content that people open on purpose, such as help on a field or a small set of filters. A popover is a small dialog anchored to its trigger: it can have a header with a title, a body and a footer with up to 2 actions.

## When to use

### Use a popover when

- The floating content holds interactive controls, such as a small filter panel.
- Contextual help, filters, onboarding tips or actions need a panel anchored to a control.
- Optional help is longer than 2 sentences, or has a link, and people open it on purpose with a labelled button such as More information.

### Don't use a popover when

- The content is a short label that shows on hover or focus and is not interactive. Use Tooltip.
- The task needs a blocking dialog, such as a confirmation. Use Modal.
- The content is long or has several steps. Put it on a full page and link to it.
- People choose from a list of actions. Use Menu.
- The content must always be visible. Put it on the page.

<DocsAnatomy>

1. **Arrow**

   A 22 x 12 point in the popover fill that points at the trigger. It follows the trigger along the edge, staying 20 from a corner. On by default. In code: showArrow.

2. **Icon**

   An optional 16px decorative icon before the title. In code: PopoverHeader icon.

3. **Title**

   Says what the popover is about, in body sm medium. It is a heading, level 2 by default, and names the dialog. In code: PopoverHeader title.

4. **Close button**

   An xs ghost icon button named Close popover. It shows only when you pass onClose. In code: onClose.

5. **Body**

   Any content, such as text, fields or a list. In code: PopoverContent.

6. **Footer label**

   Optional text for progress or context, such as 1 of 3, in caption regular. In code: PopoverFooter label.

7. **Actions**

   Up to 2 xs buttons at the end of the footer, 8 apart, the main one last. In code: buttons as PopoverFooter children.

</DocsAnatomy>

The trigger is the pressable first child of PopoverTrigger, usually a Button. The surface is Popover, with PopoverDialog inside it. Dividers sit below the header and above the footer.

Header and footer are optional. A popover for contextual help can have a body only.

## Content guidelines

### Title

Name the title in a few words, in sentence case, with no full stop. Leave the title out for a single line of help.

| Do              | Don't                                                                                         |
| :-------------- | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Processing time | Information about processing times for applications. (too long, and a title has no full stop) |
| Filters         | Info (vague)                                                                                  |
|                 | Note (vague)                                                                                  |

### Body

Keep the body short: one idea in 2 or 3 short sentences, or a few fields. End every sentence with a full stop. Link to a full page for more. Long content and multi-step forms belong on a full page.

| Do                                                              | Don't                                                                              |
| :-------------------------------------------------------------- | :--------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Most applications are processed within 5 working days.          | A popover with 5 paragraphs of eligibility rules (belongs on the page)             |
| Your reference number is in the email we sent when you applied. | Before you apply, followed by 5 sentences of steps (too long; link to a full page) |

### Footer actions

Use at most 2 actions, each starting with a verb, with the main action last at the end of the footer. Use Close for a popover that holds no unsaved input, and Done to finish a task with nothing left to save.

| Do                  | Don't                                         |
| :------------------ | :-------------------------------------------- |
| Clear, then Apply   | Apply, then Clear (the main action goes last) |
| Previous, then Next | OK (does not say what happens)                |

### Footer label

Use the footer label for progress or context, written with numerals. Leave it empty otherwise.

| Do     | Don't                                             |
| :----- | :------------------------------------------------ |
| 1 of 3 | Step one out of three (write numbers as numerals) |

### Trigger label

Label the trigger with what the popover holds, using the same words as its title when it has one.

| Do               | Don't                                             |
| :--------------- | :------------------------------------------------ |
| Filters          | Click for more (says the gesture, not what opens) |
| More information | Info icon with no name (unnamed trigger)          |

### Built-in strings and Dhivehi

Popover has two built-in strings. The header close button is named Close popover (ޕޮޕްއޯވަރ ބަންދުކުރައްވާ in Dhivehi). If you change it with closeButtonLabel, give a useful name in both languages, such as Close filters. The visually hidden Dismiss buttons have no prop and stay in English on Dhivehi pages.

Write Dhivehi titles and body text natively, keep them as short as the English, and check that Dhivehi titles still fit the header. Wrap long text rather than cutting it.

- A popover has 12 placements: top, bottom, left and right, each centred or aligned to one end. Bottom, centred, is the default.
- When the alignment should mirror in Dhivehi, use the start and end placements, which follow the reading direction, rather than left and right.
- The popover flips or moves when space is short, and stays 12 from the edge of the screen.
- Place it so it does not cover its trigger or the field it explains.

## Arrow

Keep the arrow on, the default, whenever the trigger is small or sits among other controls: it ties the popover to its trigger. Turn showArrow off when the popover lines up with a wide trigger, such as a filter bar. With the arrow the popover sits 12 from the trigger; without it, 8.

- **Body only**: for contextual help on a field or a term. Name the dialog with aria-label on PopoverDialog, such as Help with National ID number.
- **Header and body**: the title names the popover. The close button shows only when you pass onClose.
- **Footer**: add a footer label and up to 2 actions. Footer actions close the popover only when your code closes it; use the close function that PopoverDialog passes to its children.
- **Dividers**: hide the header or footer divider with showDivider.
- **Width**: set the panel width with className. The surface is 274 wide in the design file.
- **Size**: keep the body to a few lines or a few fields. If it needs scrolling or more than 2 actions, move the content to a full page.

## Behaviour

- **Opening**: a popover opens on press, never on hover. PopoverTrigger needs a pressable child such as Button.
- **Closing**: it closes on Escape, on its close button or on a press outside. Focus returns to the trigger.
- **Modal layer**: by default, while a popover is open, content outside it is hidden from screen readers and a press outside closes it. Set isNonModal for a panel that must stay open while people use the page.
- **Controlled**: control the open state with isOpen and onOpenChange on PopoverTrigger, or start open with defaultOpen.
- **Scrolling**: the body scrolls when the content is taller than the space. Keep scrolling inside the popover so it does not move the page behind.

## Tokens

| Intent    | Fill and arrow         | Text and icon                                                                | Dividers                      |
| :-------- | :--------------------- | :--------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :---------------------------- |
| primary   | `popover/bg-primary`   | `popover/fg-on-primary`                                                      | `popover/border-on-primary`   |
| secondary | `popover/bg-secondary` | `popover/fg-on-secondary`                                                    | `popover/border-on-secondary` |
| tertiary  | `popover/bg-tertiary`  | Title `text/base/strong`, icon `icon/base/strong`, label `text/base/DEFAULT` | `border/base/muted`           |

A popover is a middle layer: give it a shadow stronger than a raised card and lighter than a dialog, and a subtle border as well. In dark mode the lighter raised surface and the border carry the elevation, because shadows barely show on dark backgrounds. Buttons inside keep their own focus ring in `border/focus`. On the primary and secondary fills, content you add must use the treatment for coloured surfaces: text reaches 4.5:1 and parts reach 3:1 against the fill, focus rings included. The `border/focus` ring on these fills was not checked; see "Missing or unverified".

## Usage

<div className="preview mb-4 rounded-lg border p-4">
  <PopoverUsageDemo client:visible />
</div>

```tsx
<PopoverTrigger>
  <Button intent="secondary">Account</Button>
  <Popover placement="bottom" className="w-[274px]">
    <PopoverDialog>
      {({ close }) => (
        <>
          <PopoverHeader title="Account" icon={<UserIcon aria-hidden />} onClose={close} />
          <PopoverContent>
            <p className="type-body-sm-regular">Signed in as Aishath Ali.</p>
          </PopoverContent>
          <PopoverFooter label="Step 1 of 2">
            <Button intent="primary" size="xs" onPress={close}>
              Next
            </Button>
          </PopoverFooter>
        </>
      )}
    </PopoverDialog>
  </Popover>
</PopoverTrigger>
```

`PopoverTrigger` owns open state and restores focus to the opener. Use controlled `isOpen` / `onOpenChange` when routing or async guards manage visibility. Size the surface with `className` — Figma draws it 274px wide.

## Intents

`tertiary` is the default raised surface. `primary` uses the brand surface and `secondary` the inverse surface. Header, footer, close button, and arrow follow the intent.

<div className="preview mb-4 rounded-lg border p-4">
  <PopoverIntentsDemo client:visible />
</div>

```tsx
<Popover intent="primary">
  …
  <PopoverFooter label="Label">
    <Button intent="secondary" size="xs">
      Next
    </Button>
  </PopoverFooter>
</Popover>
```

Pair footer actions with the surface: `secondary` buttons on `primary` popovers, and `primary` buttons on `secondary` and `tertiary` popovers.

## API Reference

Popover is a Veli surface on React Aria [`DialogTrigger`](https://react-spectrum.adobe.com/react-aria/DialogTrigger.html), [`Popover`](https://react-spectrum.adobe.com/react-aria/Popover.html), and [`Dialog`](https://react-spectrum.adobe.com/react-aria/Dialog.html). Props below follow that stack.

### Popover

Veli-specific props for the positioned surface.

| Prop        | Type                                     | Default       | Description                                                  |
| :---------- | :--------------------------------------- | :------------ | :----------------------------------------------------------- |
| `intent`    | `'primary' \| 'secondary' \| 'tertiary'` | `'tertiary'`  | Surface treatment for the popover and its parts.             |
| `showArrow` | `boolean`                                | `true`        | Renders the directional arrow.                               |
| `className` | `string` or render function              | -             | Classes on the positioned root, such as width or max height. |
| `lang`      | `string`                                 | from provider | Language of the portaled surface.                            |
| `dir`       | `string`                                 | from provider | Direction of the portaled surface.                           |
| `children`  | `ReactNode`                              | -             | Usually a `PopoverDialog`.                                   |

### React Aria Popover

React Aria’s headless [`Popover`](https://react-spectrum.adobe.com/react-aria/Popover.html#popover). These props pass through, with Veli defaults for `offset` and `arrowBoundaryOffset`. The React Aria docs cover the rest.

| Prop                        | Type                         | Default               | Description                                   |
| :-------------------------- | :--------------------------- | :-------------------- | :-------------------------------------------- |
| `placement`                 | `Placement`                  | `'bottom'`            | Preferred physical or logical side/alignment. |
| `offset`                    | `number`                     | `12` / `8`            | Gap from the trigger with or without arrow.   |
| `crossOffset`               | `number`                     | `0`                   | Offset along the other axis.                  |
| `arrowBoundaryOffset`       | `number`                     | `20`                  | Minimum arrow inset from the surface edge.    |
| `shouldFlip`                | `boolean`                    | `true`                | Flip when the preferred side lacks space.     |
| `containerPadding`          | `number`                     | `12`                  | Minimum distance from the container boundary. |
| `isNonModal`                | `boolean`                    | `false`               | Allow interaction outside while open.         |
| `isKeyboardDismissDisabled` | `boolean`                    | `false`               | Prevent Escape from closing.                  |
| `triggerRef`                | `RefObject<Element \| null>` | from `PopoverTrigger` | Anchor for standalone positioning.            |

### PopoverTrigger

React Aria’s headless [`DialogTrigger`](https://react-spectrum.adobe.com/react-aria/DialogTrigger.html). Veli forwards its props unchanged.

| Prop           | Type                        | Default | Description                                    |
| :------------- | :-------------------------- | :------ | :--------------------------------------------- |
| `isOpen`       | `boolean`                   | -       | Controlled open state.                         |
| `defaultOpen`  | `boolean`                   | `false` | Uncontrolled initial open state.               |
| `onOpenChange` | `(isOpen: boolean) => void` | -       | Called when the open state changes.            |
| `children`     | `ReactNode`                 | -       | A pressable trigger followed by the `Popover`. |

### Composition

Assemble the dialog with `PopoverDialog`, `PopoverHeader`, `PopoverContent`, and `PopoverFooter`.

| Part             | Notes                                                                               |
| :--------------- | :---------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `PopoverDialog`  | Dialog semantics and focus. `children` may be a function that receives `{ close }`. |
| `PopoverHeader`  | Optional icon, title heading, close button, and divider.                            |
| `PopoverContent` | Padded body that grows and can scroll.                                              |
| `PopoverFooter`  | Optional divider, caption label, and end-aligned actions.                           |

### PopoverHeader

| Prop               | Type                         | Default   | Description                                                  |

## Accessibility

- Provide one dialog name: `PopoverHeader` `title`, or `aria-label` / `aria-labelledby` on `PopoverDialog`.
- Focus moves into the dialog on open and returns to the trigger on close. Escape dismisses unless `isKeyboardDismissDisabled` is set.
- Icon-only triggers need an `aria-label`. Header icons and dividers are decorative.
- Entry and exit fade, scale, and slide from the trigger. Reduced motion keeps the fade and removes the movement.

## RTL and Language

The overlay inherits `lang` and `dir` from `VeliProvider`. Logical placements, the arrow side, and header and footer alignment follow `dir`. On `lang="dv"`, the default close button label is `ޕޮޕްއޯވަރ ބަންދުކުރައްވާ`.

<DocsTab id="accessibility">

How the popover works with keyboards, screen readers and assistive settings. The popover is a dialog anchored to its trigger, built on React Aria DialogTrigger, Popover and Dialog.

## Keyboard

| Key                 | Action                                              |
| :------------------ | :-------------------------------------------------- |
| Enter or Space      | On the trigger, opens the popover                   |
| Tab and Shift + Tab | Move between controls inside the popover            |
| Escape              | Closes the popover and returns focus to the trigger |

Don't set isKeyboardDismissDisabled. Keep Escape and the close button working, so keyboard users can always leave the popover.

## Screen readers

- Wrap the content in PopoverDialog to get dialog behaviour and focus management.
- Give every popover one name: the PopoverHeader title, which is a heading that names the dialog, or aria-label on PopoverDialog when there is no header.
- The trigger reports whether the popover is open and which dialog it controls.
- While a popover is open, content outside it is hidden from screen readers, unless isNonModal is set.
- The header icon and the dividers are decorative.
- On phones, visually hidden Dismiss buttons at the start and end of the popover let screen reader users close it without Escape. They read Dismiss in English on Dhivehi pages.

## Focus

Focus moves into the popover when it opens and returns to the trigger when it closes. The close button and footer buttons show the Veli focus ring on keyboard focus.

## Trigger name

Give an icon-only trigger an accessible name that says what opens, such as More information. Don't name it after the picture, such as Info icon.

## Open on press

Open a popover on press, never on hover. For text that shows on hover or focus, use Tooltip.

## Target size

The header close button is 28 x 28, above the 24 x 24 minimum target but under the 44 x 44 recommended for touch. Footer buttons follow the Button sizes you choose.

## Contrast

Title, body text, footer label and header icon have a contrast of 6:1 or more in every intent in both colour modes. Keep footer buttons readable on the fill by pairing them with the intent as described under Intents.

## Zoom

Keep the popover inside the viewport at high zoom. Set a suitable width and let it flip when space is short rather than overlap its trigger.

## Right to left

Set the language with VeliProvider (language dv). The popover overlay takes the language and direction, the close button gets its Dhivehi name, and logical placements, the arrow side and the header and footer alignment follow the direction. Pass Dhivehi titles, body text and actions yourself.
