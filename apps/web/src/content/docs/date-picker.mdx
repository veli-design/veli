# DatePicker

<DocsTab id="overview">

A date picker lets people enter a date by typing it or by choosing it from a calendar. It combines editable day, month and year segments with a calendar button that opens a popover. Use it when people must give one specific date, such as an appointment or a document's expiry date, and seeing the month helps.

## When to use

### Use a date picker when

- People must give one specific date, such as an appointment or a date of issue.
- Seeing the month helps, such as booking within the next few weeks.
- The calendar must show minimum, maximum or unavailable dates.
- People may type the date into its segments or pick it from the calendar.
- A form needs compact date selection with a calendar available on demand.

### Don't use a date picker when

- The date is one people know by heart, such as a date of birth. Prefill it, or use a date field. A date picker is acceptable for a date of birth only in English forms, because its calendar opens near today and its year list shows only 10 years either side.
- The calendar must stay visible for scheduling or browsing availability. Use a calendar on the page.
- People need segment-by-segment typing without a popover. Use a date field.
- You need a date range. Use two date pickers labelled Start date and End date.
- Only the month and year matter, such as a card expiry. Use Select or Input.
- People pick from a few fixed dates, such as three open appointment days. Use a Radio group with each date written in full, such as Thursday 22 October 2026.
- The date is shown, not edited. Show it as text, written as 21 October 2026.
- Browser autofill matters for a date of birth. Use three Inputs with bday-day, bday-month and bday-year autocomplete.

<DocsAnatomy>

1. **Label**

   Names the date people give, and the whole date group for screen readers. Body sm regular at sizes sm and md, body md regular at lg. In code it is label.

2. **Info icon**

   A 16px icon button that opens a tooltip with extra help. Screen readers announce it as More information unless infoTooltipAriaLabel is set. In code it is infoTooltip.

3. **Optional**

   Optional after the label, shown with isOptional. It hides while isRequired is on, so it never shows with the asterisk.

4. **Required indicator**

   An asterisk in `text/danger/muted`. It shows while isRequired and showRequiredIndicator are both on.

5. **Field**

   The input container, which screen readers treat as one group. It is 36, 44 or 52 high by size. The default appearance has a bottom border, outline a full border.

6. **Date segments**

   Day, month and year, separate parts people type into or change with the arrow keys. The separators use `text/base/subtle`. In code it is DateSegment.

7. **Calendar button**

   A 20px calendar icon button at the end of the field that opens the popover. Screen readers announce it as Open calendar unless calendarButtonLabel is set.

8. **Helper text**

   A hint below the field. While isInvalid is on, errorMessage replaces it. In code it is description.

9. **Calendar popover**

   Opens 4 below the field, aligned to its start, with month navigation, the month grid and a Today button unless showTodayButton is off. In code it is Calendar.

</DocsAnatomy>

## Content guidelines

### Label

Name the date as a short noun phrase in sentence case, without a colon or a full stop. The label also names the whole date group for screen readers.

| Do                   | Don't                                                  |
| :------------------- | :----------------------------------------------------- |
| Appointment date     | Date (does not say which date)                         |
| Date of issue        | Date: (bare and ends with a colon)                     |
| Passport expiry date | Select Appointment Date (an instruction in title case) |

When the picker shows a date and time, name both parts: Appointment date and time.

### Helper text

Add helper text only when the label needs context, in one sentence with no full stop. Don't write the format, because the segments already show it; give the rule instead.

| Do                                          | Don't                                                                                                                     |
| :------------------------------------------ | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Sunday to Thursday, within the next 60 days | Weekdays only. (weekdays is unclear where the weekend is Friday and Saturday, and a one-sentence hint takes no full stop) |
| As printed on your passport                 | DD/MM/YYYY (the segments already show the format)                                                                         |

### Error messages

The error replaces the helper text, so the error must carry the rule. Say which rule failed, in the words of the label:

- Empty: Enter &#123;date name&#125;.
- Incomplete: &#123;date name&#125; must include a &#123;day, month or year&#125;.
- Year the wrong length: Year must include 4 numbers.
- Impossible date: &#123;date name&#125; must be a real date.
- Wrong side of today: &#123;date name&#125; must be in the past, must be today or in the past, or must be in the future.
- Outside a range: &#123;date name&#125; must be after, before or between &#123;date&#125;, with the date written as 21 October 2026.
- Time: Enter &#123;time name&#125; in 24-hour format, like 14:30.

| Do                                                               | Don't                                                                                                                                  |
| :--------------------------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Enter your appointment date                                      | Invalid date (gives no fix)                                                                                                            |
| Appointment date must be a real date                             | Pick an appointment date within the allowed window. (Pick instead of Choose, the window is not named, and a full stop on one sentence) |
| Appointment date must be between 23 October and 21 December 2026 | Choose a date from Sunday to Thursday in the next 60 days. (full stop on a one-sentence error, and weekday is unclear in the Maldives) |

### Times

Show times in 24-hour form: set granularity to hour or minute and hourCycle to 24, so times read 09:00 and 13:30.

| Do                               | Don't                    |
| :------------------------------- | :----------------------- |
| Appointment date and time, 13:30 | 10:00 AM (12-hour clock) |

### Dates in text

Add the weekday before the date, with no comma, when people need to plan around it: Wednesday 21 October 2026.

### Optional or required

Mark whichever is rarer in the form: Optional with isOptional when most fields are required, or the asterisk with isRequired when most are optional. Code never shows Optional and the asterisk on one field.

### Built-in strings

The date picker has these built-in strings: Optional, More information (infoTooltipAriaLabel), Open calendar (calendarButtonLabel), Today (todayButtonLabel) and a hidden dismiss button. When a form has more than one date picker, override calendarButtonLabel so each button names its date, such as Open calendar for appointment date, and pass the Dhivehi text yourself under language dv. Two buttons both named Open calendar sound the same to a screen reader user. Optional and the dismiss button can't be changed.

### Dhivehi copy

Veli does not translate the description and errorMessage, so write both languages. Write Dhivehi the way a Dhivehi speaker would say it and keep it as short as the English.

- Use **sm** (36 high) in dense layouts, such as filters, tables and toolbars.
- Use **md** (44 high) in most forms. It is the default in code.
- Use **lg** (52 high) for touch-first forms and pages that ask one question.

The calendar popover is the same at every size. The field keeps its height in Dhivehi; the label row grows (see Language and direction). Match the size of the other fields in the form.

## Calendar popover

- The calendar button opens the popover with focus on the selected date, or on today when there is no value. Picking a date closes it; set shouldCloseOnSelect to false to keep it open.
- The popover holds the single-date calendar, not a range calendar.
- The header has previous and next month buttons and a year button. The year list shows 10 years either side of the current view.
- A Today button sits at the foot and jumps to today. It shows by default; set showTodayButton to false to hide it. It is disabled when today is unavailable.
- Weeks start on Sunday.
- The popover is 316 wide, opens 4 below the field and clamps to the viewport on narrow phones. Make sure narrow viewports have room for it.

## Behaviour

- **Typing**: each segment takes digits and moves on when full. Up and Down change the value of the focused segment.
- **Limits**: minValue and maxValue set the allowed range. Dates outside it are disabled in the calendar and invalid when typed.
- **Unavailable dates**: isDateUnavailable marks single dates, such as public holidays, as unavailable. In the calendar they stay focusable, show a red line through the number and can't be chosen.
- **Validation**: validationBehavior is native by default, so the field takes part in form validation. errorMessage shows while isInvalid is on.
- **Inside a Form**: the Form's default validationBehavior aria shows an error while people type. Set validationBehavior="native" on the Form to check dates when people submit.
- **Form value**: control it with value and onChange, or leave it uncontrolled with defaultValue. Give it a name so the form submits an ISO date string, such as 2026-10-22. Convert with isoDateToDateValue and dateValueToIsoDate.
- **Width**: the field fills its container. Constrain the parent, not the field.

The field keeps its English height, but the label row grows: in Storybook it is 36 high at sm and md and 40 at lg, against 28, 28 and 32 in English (the label line is 28 or 32, plus 8 below). See Missing or unverified.
- The calendar shows Dhivehi month and weekday names, such as ނޮވެމްބަރު and އާދީއްތަ.

## Validation

When `isInvalid` is set, `errorMessage` replaces the helper text. `minValue`, `maxValue`, and `isDateUnavailable` limit selectable dates.

<div className="preview mb-4 rounded-lg border p-4">
  <DatePickerValidationDemo client:visible />
</div>

```tsx

```

Give the field a `name` to submit its completed value as an ISO string. Use `value` and `onChange` for a controlled value, or `defaultValue` for an initial value. `granularity` adds editable time segments for date-time values.

When the form stores ISO `yyyy-MM-dd` strings, convert with `isoDateToDateValue` / `dateValueToIsoDate` from `@veli-design/react/form`. You can also hold `DateValue` objects in form state. See [Forms](/patterns/forms/) for form-library integration.

## Accessibility

Each date segment is individually editable and focusable. The calendar button is a separate control. Tab reaches the field and button; arrow keys and typing edit segments; Escape closes the calendar and returns focus. Helper and error content are linked to the picker. The Today action remains disabled when today's date is unavailable.

## RTL and Language

Set `language` on `VeliProvider`. English and caller-supplied locales keep their date format. Dhivehi uses native RTL labels and calendar text, with an English Gregorian formatter scoped to its editable field to keep Latin day/month/year segments stable across browser engines. The numeric field runs LTR and aligns to the right. Calendar navigation mirrors in RTL, while date values remain semantic dates rather than formatted strings.

How the date picker works with keyboards, screen readers and zoom, and what you still need to do. It is built on the React Aria date picker.

## Keyboard

In the field:

| Key                                   | Action                                                  |
| :------------------------------------ | :------------------------------------------------------ |
| Tab                                   | Moves through the segments, then to the calendar button |
| Left and Right arrows                 | Move between segments, mirrored in Dhivehi              |
| Up and Down arrows                    | Change the value of the focused segment                 |
| Digits                                | Type a value; focus moves on when the segment is full   |
| Enter or Space on the calendar button | Opens the calendar popover                              |

In the calendar popover:

| Key                                   | Action                                                      |
| :------------------------------------ | :---------------------------------------------------------- |
| Arrow keys                            | Move between days                                           |
| Enter or Space                        | Picks the focused day                                       |
| Page Up and Page Down                 | Previous or next month                                      |
| Shift + Page Up and Shift + Page Down | Previous or next year                                       |
| Home and End                          | First or last day of the month                              |
| Escape                                | Closes the popover and returns focus to the calendar button |

Home and End move to the first and last day of the month, as React Aria does, where the APG date picker moves to the start and end of the week. Veli keeps this on purpose.

## Screen readers

- The visible label names the date group. Every date picker needs one; never hide it.
- Each segment is a spinbutton named day, month or year with the label.
- The calendar button is named Open calendar, and reports that it opens a dialog. The icon is hidden.
- The popover is a dialog labelled by the field's label. Each day cell is named with the full date, such as Thursday, 12 November 2026, and today is announced as Today.
- Helper and error text are linked to the picker.

## Focus

Keyboard focus on a date segment or the calendar button shows a 2px gap, then a 2px ring in `border/focus`, around the field only. The ring sits outside the field, so focusing doesn't change its size or move the label or helper text. A pointer click shows the active state without the ring. While the ring shows, outline drops its border and default keeps its bottom border. When the popover opens, focus moves to the selected date, or to today.

## Disabled and read only

A disabled date picker is muted, skipped by Tab, and its calendar can't open. A read-only date picker keeps its value readable, but the calendar button is disabled. Both keep the React Aria semantics for their state.

## Errors

The error text shows as well as the red border, so colour is not the only signal.

## Target size

Calendar day cells are 44px square, above the 24px minimum target size. The calendar button is 20 x 20 and the info button 16 x 16, below the 24px minimum; see Missing or unverified.

## Contrast

Against the resting field fill, the Figma page gives the value 11.8:1 in light and 10.3:1 in dark. Helper text in `text/base/muted` measures 4.96:1 in light and 5.23:1 in dark in Storybook, which meets 4.5:1. The placeholder uses `text/base/inactive`, about 2.7:1 in light and 2.3:1 in dark, so it doesn't meet 4.5:1. Never let the placeholder be the label or carry instructions.

## Zoom and narrow screens

The popover clamps to the viewport width on narrow phones. Keep it inside the viewport at high zoom.

## Right to left

Set the language with VeliProvider (language dv). It gives the popover the active language and direction. Day cell names, the grid label and segment names stay in English under language dv; see Missing or unverified.
