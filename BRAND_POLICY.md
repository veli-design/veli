# Veli Design brand and asset scope

The MIT licence covers original software and code examples; CC BY 4.0
covers original non-software documentation and original illustrative works
created for Veli Design. Animation code remains MIT. Unclassified or
third-party assets retain their own terms. `LICENSING.md` defines the boundary. Neither grant
relicenses excluded brand/government assets or third-party material.

The following materials are excluded from both original-work grants:

- Veli Design names, logos and brand artwork, including `apps/web/public/favicon*.svg`,
  `apps/web/public/images/veli-mark.svg` and `veli-design-*.svg` in
  `apps/web/public/images/marketing/`.
- Government and organisation identifiers: `govmv-logo*.svg`, `maldives-flag.svg`
  and `example-organization-emblem.svg` in both apps' `public/blocks/` directories;
  `govmv-wordmark.svg`, `government-emblem*.svg`, and `mindco-wordmark.svg`
  in `apps/web/public/images/marketing/`.
- Font binaries in `apps/web/public/fonts/` and `apps/storybook/public/fonts/`.
  These require their own upstream licences and notices; font-family names in
  the tokens do not distribute or license fonts.
- Phosphor icons are supplied by `@phosphor-icons/react` under MIT. The complete
  upstream copyright and licence notice is preserved in `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`
  and must accompany redistributed Phosphor artwork. The MIT licence grants no
  trademark or endorsement rights.
- Other public artwork until its original-project or third-party classification
  has been recorded. Original illustrative works follow
  CC BY 4.0, not the reserved-logo policy; code implementing them follows MIT.
